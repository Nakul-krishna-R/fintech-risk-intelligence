Item 1.02. Termination of a Material Definitive Agreement.
On January 29, 2021, The Home Depot, Inc. (the “Company”) terminated its $3.5 billion 364-day revolving credit facility agreement dated as of March 23, 2020 between the Company as borrower, the banks party thereto, and JPMorgan Chase Bank as administrative agent. There were no borrowings under the credit facility, and the Company determined that the credit facility was no longer necessary.
