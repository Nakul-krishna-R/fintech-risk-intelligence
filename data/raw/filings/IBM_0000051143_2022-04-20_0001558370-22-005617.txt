EXPLANATORY NOTE
​
The registrant is furnishing this Amendment No. 1 to its Current Report on Form 8-K furnished on April 19, 2022 (the Original Form 8-K) to furnish an updated slide number 30 of Exhibit 99.3 of the Original Form 8-K to correct a typographical error in which the time period attributed to the expectation for pre-tax margin improvement was labeled “from 1Q22 to 2Q22” instead of the correct time period of “Yr/Yr from 2Q21 to 2Q22.” This Amendment No. 1 is solely to furnish the updated slide number 30 of Exhibit 99.3 of the Original Form 8-K to correct such typographical error. No other changes have been made to the Original Form 8-K.
​
Item 7.01. Regulation FD Disclosure.
​
The updated slide number 30 of Exhibit 99.3 of the Original Form 8-K for IBM’s Chairman and Chief Executive Officer Arvind Krishna and Chief Financial Officer Jim Kavanaugh’s first-quarter 2022 earnings presentation on April 19, 2022, is Exhibit 99.1 to this Form 8-K.
​
The information in this Item 7.01, including the corresponding Exhibit 99.1, is being furnished with the Commission and shall not be deemed “filed” for purposes of Section 18 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934.
