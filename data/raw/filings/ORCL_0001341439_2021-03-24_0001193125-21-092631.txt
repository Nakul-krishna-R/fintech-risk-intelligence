Item 8.01

Other Events

Issuance of $15 Billion Aggregate Principal Amount of Notes

On March 24, 2021, Oracle Corporation (“Oracle”) consummated the issuance and sale of $2,750,000,000 aggregate principal amount of its 1.650% Notes due 2026, $2,000,000,000 aggregate principal amount of its 2.300% Notes due 2028, $3,250,000,000 aggregate principal amount of its 2.875% Notes due 2031, $2,250,000,000 aggregate principal amount of its 3.650% Notes due 2041, $3,250,000,000 aggregate principal amount of its 3.950% Notes due 2051 and $1,500,000,000 aggregate principal amount of its 4.100% Notes due 2061 (collectively, the “Notes”), pursuant to an underwriting agreement dated March 22, 2021 among Oracle and BofA Securities, Inc., Citigroup Global Markets Inc., Deutsche Bank Securities Inc., J.P. Morgan Securities LLC and Wells Fargo Securities, LLC, as representatives of the several underwriters named therein. The Notes will be issued pursuant to an Indenture dated as of January 13, 2006 (the “Indenture”) among Oracle (formerly known as Ozark Holding Inc.), Oracle Systems Corporation (formerly known as Oracle Corporation) and Citibank, N.A., as amended by the First Supplemental Indenture dated as of May 9, 2007 (the “First Supplemental Indenture”) among Oracle, Citibank, N.A. and The Bank of New York Mellon Trust Company, N.A. (formerly known as The Bank of New York Trust Company, N.A.), as trustee, and an officers’ certificate issued pursuant thereto.
