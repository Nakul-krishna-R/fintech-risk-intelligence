ITEM 7.01 REGULATION FD DISCLOSURE

On January 9, 2024, The Procter & Gamble Company (the “Company”) announced that its Board of Directors declared a quarterly dividend of $0.9407 per
share on the Common Stock and on the Series A and Series B ESOP Convertible Class A Preferred Stock of the Company, payable on or after February 15, 2024, to Common Stock shareholders of record at the close of business on January 19, 2024,
and to Series A and Series B ESOP Convertible Class A Preferred Stock shareholders of record at the start of business on January 19, 2024.
