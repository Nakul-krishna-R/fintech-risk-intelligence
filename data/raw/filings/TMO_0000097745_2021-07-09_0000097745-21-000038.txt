Item 5.03 Amendments to Articles of Incorporation or Bylaws; Change in Fiscal Year.

Amendment of By-Laws

(the “Company”) amended and restated the Company’s By-Laws, effective immediately, to reduce the percentage of shareholders required to call a special meeting of stockholders from 25 percent to 15 percent.
