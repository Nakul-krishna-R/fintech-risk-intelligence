Item 5.02.

Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain Officers; Compensatory Arrangements of Certain Officers.
Appointment of Charles D. Baker as independent director
On November 3, 2023, UnitedHealth Group Incorporated (“the “Company”) issued a press release announcing the appointment of Charles D. Baker to serve as an independent director of the Company, effective October 30, 2023. Mr. Baker was also appointed to serve on both the Audit and Finance Committee and the Health and Clinical Practice Policies Committee of the Company’s Board of Directors. A copy of the press release is attached hereto as Exhibit 99.1 and is incorporated herein by reference.
Mr. Baker will receive compensation for his services as a director in accordance with the Company’s standard compensation program for non-employee directors as amended from time to time, the material terms of which are described in the Company’s 2023 proxy statement.
In accordance with the Company’s customary practice, the Company is entering into its standard form of indemnification agreement with Mr. Baker, which requires the Company to indemnify him against certain liabilities that may arise as a result of his status or service as a director. The foregoing description is qualified in its entirety by the full text of the form of Indemnification Agreement, which was filed as Exhibit 10.1 to the Company’s Current Report on Form 8-K filed on July 1, 2015.
There are no arrangements or understandings between Mr. Baker and any other person pursuant to which Mr. Baker was appointed to serve on the Board of Directors. Mr. Baker has no direct or indirect material interest in any transaction required to be disclosed pursuant to Item 404(a) of Regulation S-K.
