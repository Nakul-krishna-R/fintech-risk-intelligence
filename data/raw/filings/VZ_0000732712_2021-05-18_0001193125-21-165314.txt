(b)

The proposal regarding the Advisory Vote to Approve Executive Compensation was approved with 2,556,473,582 votes for, 207,460,171 votes against, 22,876,844 abstentions and 676,534,032 broker
non-votes.

(c)

The appointment of Ernst & Young LLP as independent registered public accounting firm for 2021 was ratified with 3,275,839,425 votes for, 170,659,086 votes against and 16,846,118 abstentions.

(d)

The shareholder proposal regarding Shareholder Action by Written Consent was defeated with 974,294,087 votes for, 1,782,735,591 votes against, 29,780,919 abstentions and 676,534,032 broker
non-votes.

(e)

non-votes.

(f)

The shareholder proposal regarding Shareholder Ratification of Annual Equity Awards was defeated with 1,051,634,546 votes for, 1,706,772,519 votes against, 28,403,532 abstentions and 676,534,032 broker
non-votes.
