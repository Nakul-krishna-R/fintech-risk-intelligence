th
interest in a share of the Series O Preferred Stock (the “Series O Depositary Shares”). After giving effect to the redemption on the Redemption Date, no shares of the Series O Preferred Stock or the Series O Depositary Shares will remain outstanding.

th
interest in a share of the Series X Preferred Stock (the “Series X Depositary Shares”). After giving effect to the redemption on the Redemption Date, no shares of the Series X Preferred Stock or the Series X Depositary Shares will remain outstanding.
