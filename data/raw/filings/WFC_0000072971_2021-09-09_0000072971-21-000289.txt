Item 8.01 Other Events.
On September 9, 2021, Wells Fargo & Company (the “Company”) issued a news release announcing it had entered into a consent order with the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency (“OCC”) regarding the Company’s loss mitigation activities. In addition, the Company announced it had entered into a separate consent order with the OCC to pay a $250 million civil money penalty regarding the Company’s loss mitigation activities and insufficient progress in addressing requirements under the Company’s April 2018 consent order with the OCC. Copies of the news release and each consent order are included as Exhibits 99.1, 99.2 and 99.3 to this report and are incorporated by reference into this Item 8.01.
